# SWABHIMAN interface

The marketplace takes its visual cues from a civic directory. A deep indigo introduction gives the local hiring and work actions a clear starting point. A ruled trade index lets people narrow results by skill, with citron highlighting the selected trade and the primary action.

Listings, forms, messages, and booking screens use cool white surfaces, indigo controls, and a consistent icon system. Trade controls stay in a two-column index on mobile, with stable touch targets and a visible keyboard focus ring.

A looping 3D scene is shared by the home hero and the other view headings. The controls can pause it across views, and the scene stays static when the device requests reduced motion.
